ACCESSIBILITY

Designed to be usable.

Accessibility is a product and website engineering requirement, not a marketing badge.

THE END IN MIND

Let people navigate and understand this website regardless of the device or input method they use.

A visitor may be reviewing a proposal on a phone, using a keyboard at a desk or enlarging text to read it comfortably. The route to understanding TURBINE should not depend on one screen size or a perfect set of circumstances.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    Clarity is part of access

    Plain instructions help people with different experiences and abilities.

  2. 02

    Every important action needs a label

    Buttons and links should explain what will happen.

  3. 03

    A keyboard must work

    Navigation and interactions should not depend on a mouse.

  4. 04

    Text must have room to grow

    Responsive design includes zoom, reflow and readable contrast.

  5. 05

    Feedback is part of improvement

    Unverified reader and physical-device behavior remains a test obligation.
