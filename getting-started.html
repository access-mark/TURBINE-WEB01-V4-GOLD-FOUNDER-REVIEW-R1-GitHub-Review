GETTING STARTED

A deliberate path from discovery to operation.

There is no need to rush into a new system. Start with one clear outcome, understand what must change, prepare your information and make sure the next step is properly qualified.

THE END IN MIND

Leave knowing the next sensible step for your business, without committing to an unqualified trial.

If you are a solo operator, you may begin with a list of clients and a simple job flow. If you run an SME, you may start with a sales-to-cash problem. If you have multiple systems and teams, discovery must cover migration, authority and cutover.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    Tell the story of today

    Map what customers ask for and what your people do next.

  2. 02

    Agree one measurable result

    Examples: fewer forgotten jobs, clearer margins or faster collections.

  3. 03

    Blueprint the complete workflow

    Include data, people, approvals and financial consequences.

  4. 04

    Validate before changing live work

    Use qualified trials and acceptance scenarios when available.

  5. 05

    Adopt with a safe cutover

    Do not rely on an unproven import or a demo as a migration plan.

01 / 05

01 — Understand your operating model

Tell us what your business does, which systems you currently use, where information is fragmented and which outcomes matter most. We assess fit rather than promising every industry workflow immediately.

02 / 05

02 — Scope the right starting point

For straightforward businesses, that may mean a standard onboarding journey. For complex environments, a paid discovery and blueprint can define the target workflow, migration scope, integration dependencies and rollout risks.

03 / 05

03 — Request a real BusinessOS trial

A trial is intended to be an isolated instance of the actual operating system, not a scripted demonstration studio. Provisioning and access depend on TURBINE Command integration and the relevant production qualification gates.

04 / 05

04 — Validate with your own workflows

Evaluate usability, permissions, data import, reporting and the operational journey using agreed test cases. Any migration of real records must be authorised, validated and appropriately protected.

05 / 05

05 — Convert and operate

Once the trial and commercial terms are approved, TURBINE Command governs subscription entitlements, billing events and lifecycle decisions. The BusinessOS remains the place where the customer runs the business.
