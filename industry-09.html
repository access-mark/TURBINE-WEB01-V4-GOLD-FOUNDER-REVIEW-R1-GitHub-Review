From the first request to the final outcome

These steps describe the intended operating experience. Each transition keeps the people, approval, delivery and money associated with the original commitment.

  1. 01

    Keep the training client, sponsoring organisation and programme contact details associated with the enquiry.

  2. 02

    Quote the course or programme with agreed dates, group size, scope and commercial conditions.

  3. 03

    Capture intended enrolments and outstanding administrative requirements before the start date.

  4. 04

    Coordinate sessions, classrooms, equipment and capacity against the agreed programme.

  5. 05

    Assign an available trainer and preserve the expected delivery commitment.

  6. 06

    Record attendance-related administration without claiming an accredited learning-management system.

  7. 07

    Purchase training materials and venues with clear programme-related costs and approvals.

  8. 08

    Invoice the sponsoring organisation against the agreed milestone or delivery condition.

  9. 09

    Review planned versus delivered sessions, outstanding actions and programme-related financial results.

Working for yourself?

Start with a clear enquiry, next action and invoice. You should be able to understand the journey without a dedicated administrator.

Running a team or QSE?

Keep owners, delegated approvals, purchasing and collections visible as the volume and responsibility grow.

Managing a larger operation?

Coordinate cross-team controls, records, exceptions and reporting without assuming specialist sector functions are already qualified.

Make the move when your business is ready
