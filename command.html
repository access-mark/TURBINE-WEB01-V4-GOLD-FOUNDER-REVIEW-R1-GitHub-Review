COMMERCIAL CONTROL PLANE

TURBINE Command governs the lifecycle.

BusinessOS is the customer product. TURBINE Command is the central control plane responsible for governing the commercial and operational lifecycle behind it.

THE END IN MIND

Understand who controls access, subscriptions and the lifecycle of a real BusinessOS environment.

You should know who is allowed into your business environment, which services have been approved and what happens when a trial ends or a subscription changes. TURBINE Command is intended to govern those decisions outside the public marketing site.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    Identity comes first

    Each authorised person and organisation needs a trustworthy boundary.

  2. 02

    Entitlements follow agreements

    Access to capabilities must reflect valid commercial decisions.

  3. 03

    A trial is a real controlled environment

    No shared public demo is a substitute for isolation and provisioning evidence.

  4. 04

    Financial events need records

    Quotes, billing and collections require traceable authority and reconciled treatment.

  5. 05

    Recovery is part of trust

    Provisioning, monitoring and restoration must be demonstrably governed.

01 / 05

Customer and tenant governance

Coordinate customer organisations, tenant lifecycle, identity relationships, consent and the authorised boundary between customers. A trial or paid subscription must never bypass tenancy and permission controls.

02 / 05

Entitlements and subscriptions

Manage the relationship between approved plans, licensed capabilities, subscription status and the features a tenant may access. Commercial events should not rely on a public website changing entitlement flags.

03 / 05

Provisioning and recovery

Create and manage customer environments through controlled provisioning and operational processes, with appropriate auditability, backup, restoration and recovery qualification.

04 / 05

Financial and audit controls

Commercial events such as quotations, subscriptions, invoices, collections and partner attribution require authoritative records, reconciliation and auditable decision trails.

05 / 05

What the website does not do

The public site does not collect card payments, create live tenants or activate subscriptions independently. Those functions require separately deployed, tested and approved TURBINE Command services.

GOVERNED CUSTOMER LIFECYCLE

One control plane. A separate place to run the business.

The public site introduces TURBINE. Command is the intended commercial and operational authority. BusinessOS is the customer's operating environment. None of these boundaries should be blurred.

01 / WEBSITE

Discover

Understand capabilities, industries and suitability; express interest only through approved channels.

02 / COMMAND

Qualify

Govern identity, consent, plan scope, commercial decisions, entitlements and authorised provisioning.

03 / BUSINESSOS

Operate

Run customers, sales, work, stock and financial workflows inside the qualified customer environment.

04 / COMMAND

Govern

Control lifecycle changes, billing, collections, audit, support, recovery and suspension when authorised.
