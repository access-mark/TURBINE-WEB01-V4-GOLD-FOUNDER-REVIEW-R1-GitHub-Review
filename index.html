WHAT MAKES IT WORK

Five simple ideas behind a clearer working day.

  1. 01

    Begin with the work, not the software

    The first question is what you need to get done today, not which application to open.

  2. 02

    Keep the whole customer story together

  3. 03

    Make the next step obvious

    Guidance should explain where you are, what matters and how to move forward.

  4. 04

    Stay in control as responsibility grows

    One person needs simplicity; a team needs owners; a larger operation needs permissions and evidence.

  5. 05

    Move in without losing your history

    Bring essential records through governed migration and retain a route to export them.

THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Disconnected tools create
disconnected decisions.

A customer asks for something. The details go into one place, the job into another and the invoice somewhere else. You spend time finding updates instead of moving the work forward.

TURBINE is designed for full adoption as the business’s primary operating environment. A customer enquiry, an approved quotation, the work delivered, the stock or supplier cost incurred, the invoice issued and the payment received are parts of one accountable commercial story. This is not a proposition to connect a growing collection of third-party business applications.
