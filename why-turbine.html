THE DIFFERENCE

You should not have to chase your own business.

Your customers, work and money belong to the same business. So why should you spend so much time trying to connect them? TURBINE is being built to make the whole picture easier to see, from the first enquiry to the final payment.

THE END IN MIND

Spend less time looking for answers. Have more time to act on them.

A business owner knows the customer called but cannot find the latest quote. A team leader knows a job is late but cannot see what is holding it up. A director can see sales rising but struggles to connect them to cash. Different businesses, same frustration: the information exists, but it is hard to bring together.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    Software should follow your day

    Start with the customer and the work, not with a stack of apps.

  2. 02

    One promise should stay one story

    Quoting, doing, buying and billing belong in a connected operating journey.

  3. 03

    Simplicity means confidence

    People should understand what matters next without specialist training.

  4. 04

    Control should grow with you

    Owners, managers and approvers need understandable responsibility.

  5. 05

    You should own your history

    Migration and export help prevent the data from becoming a barrier to improvement.

01 / 05

The cost of working in too many places

A quotation lives in email, a project in a task board, stock in a spreadsheet, an invoice in accounting software and customer notes in someone’s phone. People spend time translating between systems, often without knowing which record is authoritative.

02 / 05

One promise. One story from beginning to end.

TURBINE’s operating model centres the original customer, commercial, operational and financial records. The intention is to reduce repeated capture and make relationships visible without manufacturing parallel sources of truth.

03 / 05

Make the work clear before making it faster

Automation is useful only when the underlying records, permissions and approvals are trustworthy. TURBINE prioritises clear workflow boundaries, explicit human decisions and traceable financial events.

04 / 05

A calmer daily experience

The application is organised around Turbine Today, Turbine Flow and contextual guidance. Users should be able to answer what needs attention, why it matters and where to go next without becoming system administrators.

05 / 05

Keep the clarity as your business grows

A new business can establish its complete operating environment from day one. An established business can migrate its records and processes through a controlled cutover. In both cases, the goal is full BusinessOS adoption rather than a permanent mixed application stack.
