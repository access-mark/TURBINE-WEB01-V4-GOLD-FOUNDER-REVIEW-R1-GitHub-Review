THE END IN MIND

See yourself in TURBINE, whether you work alone, employ ten people or manage several locations.

A gardener starts with clients and a diary; an agent starts with follow-ups and viewings; a 20-person service business needs shared assignments; a multi-site organisation needs authority, oversight and consistent records. The principle remains the same: follow the work without losing context.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    Everyone starts with a real job

    A one-person business deserves an experience that begins simply.

  2. 02

    Add people without losing the thread

    Delegation should come with ownership and visibility.

  3. 03

    As work grows, connect the functions

    Sales, delivery, stock and finance must not drift into separate versions of reality.

  4. 04

    Different scale needs different control

    A director needs approvals and evidence, not the same screen as a field worker.

  5. 05

    Keep one underlying business

    BusinessOS adoption is whole-business, with qualified configuration for each size.

QSEs

Discipline without bureaucracy

As a Qualifying Small Enterprise, you are often at the stage where buying, quoting, stock and collections need authority and traceability without slowing the team down. Under the generic B-BBEE Codes, QSE turnover is more than R10 million and less than R50 million; sector codes may differ.

The configuration and delegated responsibilities change with the organisation’s scale, but the underlying operating principle does not.

One person is responsible for sales, delivery, administration and getting paid. TURBINE should reduce the number of places that person must check.
