From the first request to the final outcome

These steps describe the intended operating experience. Each transition keeps the people, approval, delivery and money associated with the original commitment.

  1. 01

    Record the customer request, service location, access needs and urgency together.

  2. 02

    Assign work to an appropriate person and make planned dispatch visible to the team.

  3. 03

    Coordinate vehicles, tools, staff availability and other field resource conflicts.

  4. 04

    Capture arrival, job progress, service notes and evidence of what was performed.

  5. 05

    Associate subcontractor or fuel costs with the relevant service run.

  6. 06

    See which spare parts and consumables were used and need replenishment.

  7. 07

    Close the work only when required customer confirmation or supervisor approval is present.

  8. 08

    Prepare billing from confirmed service activity, not an incomplete memory of the day.

  9. 09

    Review jobs completed, revisits due, resource constraints and outstanding receipts.

Working for yourself?

Start with a clear enquiry, next action and invoice. You should be able to understand the journey without a dedicated administrator.

Running a team or QSE?

Keep owners, delegated approvals, purchasing and collections visible as the volume and responsibility grow.

Managing a larger operation?

Coordinate cross-team controls, records, exceptions and reporting without assuming specialist sector functions are already qualified.

Make the move when your business is ready
