FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions worth answering before you change systems.

Practical answers about product scope, onboarding, trials and governance.

THE END IN MIND

Resolve the questions that matter before you make a commitment to a different way of working.

If you are running payroll, clients and jobs every day, a new system is not interesting simply because it has many features. The questions are practical: Does it fit my work? What does the move involve? Can my team use it? What is ready now?

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    The whole business matters

    Do not evaluate a CRM and forget its financial and operational consequences.

  2. 02

    Ask about the difficult edge cases

    Permissions, refunds, supplier disputes and migrations matter.

  3. 03

    Understand what is qualified

    A planned feature must not be presented as a service already operating.

  4. 04

    Protect the information you own

    Import, reconciliation and export are essential questions.

  5. 05

    Know what happens after interest

    Discovery and trial enquiries are separate from entitlement and provisioning.

01 / 07

Is TURBINE accounting software or a CRM?

Neither description captures the full intended product. TURBINE is a connected BusinessOS spanning customer, commercial, operational and financial workflows, with module availability subject to qualification.

02 / 07

Is the trial a simulated demo?

No. The intended trial is an isolated instance of the actual BusinessOS, subject to provisioning and production readiness gates. This public website currently collects interest; it does not create trial tenants.

03 / 07

Can we keep our existing systems?

That depends on your requirements and the availability of qualified connectors. Some businesses may replace several systems; others may integrate selectively during a phased rollout.

04 / 07

Can TURBINE migrate thousands of records?

Bulk migration is part of the DataPort design requirement. Actual capacity, supported entities, mapping and reconciliation must be validated for each migration scope.

05 / 07

How is pricing determined?

Approved plans and prices will be published once commercially ratified. Complex migration, integration and assisted rollout may require separately scoped services.

06 / 07

Who controls subscriptions?

TURBINE Command governs the commercial lifecycle, tenant provisioning, entitlements and related financial events. The public site is not a checkout system.

07 / 07

Is the system ready for live customer data?

No general production-readiness claim is made here. Deployment is conditional on the programme’s technical, security, financial and operational qualification gates.
