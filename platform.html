THE PLATFORM

The operating model, connected.

A business is a sequence of decisions, commitments and transactions. TURBINE is designed to keep those activities in context, rather than distributing them across disconnected tools.

THE END IN MIND

Understand why one operating environment can be simpler than several well-chosen apps.

The task starts with a person who needs something. It leads to a quote, work, perhaps purchasing, an invoice and a payment. Each handoff produces a new decision. TURBINE is intended to keep the connection between those decisions visible, from sole operator to governed organisation.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    Begin with one real event

    Customer demand starts a sequence, not a collection of application tabs.

  2. 02

    Keep records related

    Quotes, jobs, purchases and invoices should reference their originating business context.

  3. 03

    Guide the people doing the work

    Give each user a clear role-appropriate starting place.

  4. 04

    Govern consequential actions

    Identity, approvals, audit and financial controls protect the operating model.

  5. 05

    Scale the whole environment

    Configuration changes with organisational complexity; the underlying story stays connected.

01 / 05

A connected operational foundation

Customer relationships, quotations, accepted commitments, delivery work, purchasing, inventory, invoicing and payment information belong to one coherent business journey. The value is not simply having multiple menu items: it is preserving the relationship between records as work moves forward.

02 / 05

Start with what matters today

Turbine Today provides an operational starting point: recorded priorities, work needing attention, approvals and next actions. Teams can act on their existing records instead of reconciling separate dashboards.

03 / 05

Follow work from beginning to end

Turbine Flow makes the stages of a business journey visible. Customer, quotation, acceptance, work, invoice and payment can be understood as connected events, with the original records retaining authority.

04 / 05

Guidance without losing control

Turbine Guide is intended to help users navigate tools, understand context and choose useful next actions. Guidance must respect permissions and distinguish recorded facts from interpretation; it is not a promise of autonomous decision-making.

05 / 05

Designed around your business

A service company, distributor and manufacturer do not operate identically. TURBINE is designed for configurable workspaces and industry-adaptive journeys, subject to qualified module availability and implementation scope.
