THE RESULT TO AIM FOR

Know whether the order can be made, what it will cost, and when you can deliver it.

From the first request to the final outcome

These steps describe the intended operating experience. Each transition keeps the people, approval, delivery and money associated with the original commitment.

  1. 01

    Record the quantity, promised date, specification and commercial terms before confirming demand.

  2. 02

    Translate the product request into required materials and known constraints before committing capacity.

  3. 03

    Request and authorise supplier purchases linked to the production need, not an unrelated spreadsheet.

  4. 04

    Confirm received material quantities, outstanding deliveries and associated stock movement.

  5. 05

    Give the work order clear stages, responsible people and a visible exception route.

  6. 06

    Capture checks, defects, rework decisions and the evidence needed for customer acceptance.

  7. 07

    Link dispatch readiness and completed delivery to the originating customer order.

  8. 08

    Understand material and job cost when preparing the invoice and reviewing gross margin.

  9. 09

    Review delayed orders, material exposure and reasons why actual results differ from the plan.

Working for yourself?

Start with a clear enquiry, next action and invoice. You should be able to understand the journey without a dedicated administrator.

Running a team or QSE?

Keep owners, delegated approvals, purchasing and collections visible as the volume and responsibility grow.

Managing a larger operation?

Coordinate cross-team controls, records, exceptions and reporting without assuming specialist sector functions are already qualified.
