ABOUT TURBINE

A more connected way to run a business.

TURBINE BusinessOS is being developed around a practical proposition: growing businesses need a coherent operational system, not an ever-expanding collection of disconnected applications.

THE END IN MIND

Know why TURBINE exists and whether its approach matches the business you want to run.

People start businesses to serve customers, build something and earn a living. Too often the business eventually spends too much time serving its software. TURBINE starts with the belief that the operating environment should support the person doing the work.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    A business is a connected story

    Customers, delivery, suppliers, people and money should make sense together.

  2. 02

    Small businesses deserve serious tools

    A sole operator should not need a back office to understand the business.

  3. 03

    Simplicity cannot mean lost control

    Clear experiences and sound permissions must coexist.

  4. 04

    Growth should not mean starting again

    The same operating principles should accommodate more people and responsibility.

  5. 05

    Evidence must precede promises

    Capabilities, trials and financial controls are offered only after relevant qualification.

01 / 04

The purpose

Make customer commitments, work, purchasing, money and decisions easier to understand in context. Give owners and teams a clearer operational picture and more deliberate next actions.

02 / 04

The design philosophy

Calm, useful interfaces. Business language rather than software jargon. Connected records rather than duplicated dashboards. Guided journeys that preserve human responsibility and clear audit boundaries.

03 / 04

The commercial philosophy

A business should be able to understand the product before buying it. Discovery, real trial access and subscription conversion are distinct steps, with TURBINE Command governing the lifecycle.

04 / 04

A South African starting point

The initial market focus is South African small and medium-sized businesses across service, commercial and operational sectors. Regulatory, industry and geographic expansion claims require separate validation.
