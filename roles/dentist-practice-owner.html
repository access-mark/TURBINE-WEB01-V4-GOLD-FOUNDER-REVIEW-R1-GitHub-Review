A BUSINESS STORY FOR DENTIST PRACTICE OWNER

Run the business administration around patient care with less confusion.

Running a practice means caring for patients while keeping administration, appointments, supplies and billing under control.

Search another occupationSee how TURBINE works
THE END IN MIND

Here is the familiar challenge.

Appointments are full, a chair needs maintenance and an account remains unsettled. The practice owner wants administrative visibility without treating sensitive clinical records as ordinary CRM data.

WHY THE OLD WAY GETS HARD

Too many moving pieces, one working day.

Practice operations include schedules, reception tasks, supplier purchasing, staff coordination and financial administration, with strict boundaries around protected health information.

FIVE PRACTICAL BELIEFS

From the first request to the final outcome

  1. 01 / 05

    Coordinate non-clinical enquiries and appointment administration

    The original request is the anchor for everything that follows. Make it clear who needs what, where the context lives and which promise was made.

  2. 02 / 05

    Assign practice tasks and equipment maintenance

    An agreement is only useful when the next person can find it. Keep scope, decisions and follow-up alongside the customer story rather than in a separate message thread.

  3. 03 / 05

    Order approved consumables and track administrative costs

    A good plan answers who owns the next action, when it is due and what must happen first. That reduces avoidable questions during a busy working day.

  4. 04 / 05

    Review appropriate financial and supplier information

    Work rarely runs exactly to plan. Keep the actual result, exceptions and client communication attached to the original request so the history makes sense.

  5. 05 / 05

    Keep clinical data and statutory obligations in qualified systems

    The end of a task should not be the end of visibility. Follow through on the money, documentation or final response, and make the next outstanding action clear.
