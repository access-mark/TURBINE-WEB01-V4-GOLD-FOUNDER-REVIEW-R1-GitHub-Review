Move the business forward, not just the data

TURBINE is not a middleware product or an implementation consultancy for keeping disconnected systems running. The customer adopts TURBINE as the primary business environment. External connections, when essential for banking, payments, statutory exchanges or migration, support the BusinessOS rather than define its value.

STEP 01

Discover

Understand the whole organisation, including people, customer journeys, financial controls and operating constraints.

STEP 02

Blueprint

Agree the end-to-end target operating model, workflows, roles, migration scope and qualification dependencies.

STEP 03

Prepare data

Use governed mapping, validation and reconciliation to prepare records for cutover. DataPort is an adoption and portability facility.

STEP 04

Configure

Establish organisation context, permissions, approved business rules, workspaces and appropriate capabilities.

STEP 05

Validate

Test the intended workflows, approvals, transaction boundaries, data quality, recovery and user experience.

STEP 06

Trial

When authorised and qualified, TURBINE Command provisions an isolated trial of the actual BusinessOS—not a demo studio.

STEP 07

Adopt

Train users, reconcile opening data and move the agreed operating processes into TURBINE with controlled cutover.

STEP 08

Operate

Run the business within BusinessOS. TURBINE Command governs tenant, subscription, entitlement and commercial lifecycle.

Three starting situations

Starting from nothing

Establish customers, workflows, finance, people and operating discipline in one environment from the outset.

Some existing software

Map the required records, validate migration and transition the business processes into TURBINE.

Many disconnected systems

Design a controlled migration and cutover programme, reconciling data and eliminating redundant operational duplication.

What the website does not promise

GitHub Pages can present product information and collect interest only after an approved lead endpoint is configured. It cannot independently provision tenants, process subscriptions or establish secure identities. Those operations belong to qualified TURBINE Command services.

ADOPTION ACCEPTANCE

Move the business only when the operating journey is proven.

Good onboarding requires much more than a migrated contact list. The blueprint should define the customer-to-cash and purchase-to-pay journeys, authority boundaries and the evidence that makes a cutover safe.

Business readiness

Agreed scope, owners, approval policies, training needs, user roles and measurable acceptance scenarios.

Data readiness

Field mapping, duplicates, opening balances, source-of-truth reconciliation, exceptions and a tested export path.

Operational readiness

Authorised identities, tenant isolation, qualified connectors, restoration testing, support and a controlled rollback decision.

No production migration, user provisioning or cutover is claimed available through this public website.
