TRIAL ACCESS

Request access to the real BusinessOS.

The intended trial is a real, isolated BusinessOS environment. The website records interest only; access can be offered after TURBINE Command provisioning and production qualification are approved.

THE END IN MIND

Know what a meaningful, safe trial would prove and why this form cannot yet create one.

A useful trial is not a shiny pretend company with dummy controls. A gardener needs to test an enquiry-to-payment journey. A distributor needs stock and finance. A larger business needs roles, isolation and approval boundaries. Each needs a governed environment and a clear acceptance test.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    Choose a real outcome to test

    Define a familiar customer-to-cash or purchase-to-pay journey.

  2. 02

    Confirm what is qualified

    Only accepted technical capabilities belong in the trial scope.

  3. 03

    Protect the real business

    Tenant isolation, identities, entitlements and data treatment must be proven.

  4. 04

    Measure acceptance visibly

    Agree how the scenario will demonstrate value and controls.

  5. 05

    Do not confuse interest with access

    The review form cannot deliver a lead or provision a tenant without approved services.
