THE END IN MIND

Never lose a client follow-up, a property-related task or the money attached to it.

An estate agent meets a buyer at a viewing while a landlord reports a maintenance problem. One person needs a follow-up, the other needs a contractor, and both need a clear history. TURBINE’s intended operating approach connects those commitments without pretending to replace specialist property regulatory systems.

THE FIVE BELIEFS

What has to be true to reach that outcome.

  1. 01

    A lead has a next action

    Record who the person is, what they need and when to follow up.

  2. 02

    Keep the relationship in context

    Owners, landlords, buyers, tenants and contractors have different roles.

  3. 03

    A viewing or service is a promise

    Appointments and maintenance requests should have ownership.

    The paper trail must make sense

A BUSINESS DAY THAT MAKES SENSE

What this looks like in property and real estate services

TURBINE’s intended operating approach connects those commitments without pretending to replace specialist property regulatory systems.

THE RESULT TO AIM FOR

Never lose a client follow-up, a property-related task or the money attached to it.

From the first request to the final outcome

These steps describe the intended operating experience. Each transition keeps the people, approval, delivery and money associated with the original commitment.

  1. 01

    A buyer enquires on a listing; retain source, preferences, responsible agent and the next contact date.

  2. 02

    Keep landlord, tenant, owner and purchaser relationships distinguished where needed by the assignment.

  3. 03

    Coordinate a viewing time, attendance and follow-up without leaving the enquiry in somebody’s phone.

  4. 04

    Turn a reported leak or broken gate into an owned maintenance request with a status and history.

  5. 05

    Compare the supplier scope and approved price before instructing the contractor.

  6. 06

    Track access, appointment, completion evidence and any return visit required to close the job.

  7. 07

    Associate correspondence and approved documents with the relevant client or property assignment.

  8. 08

    Preserve the commercial basis for a fee or commission calculation; regulated trust and transfer accounting is not claimed.

  9. 09

    Review follow-ups due, supplier costs, approved invoices and open administrative matters together.

Working for yourself?

Start with a clear enquiry, next action and invoice. You should be able to understand the journey without a dedicated administrator.

Running a team or QSE?

Keep owners, delegated approvals, purchasing and collections visible as the volume and responsibility grow.

Managing a larger operation?

Coordinate cross-team controls, records, exceptions and reporting without assuming specialist sector functions are already qualified.

Make the move when your business is ready

Discovery first establishes how work happens today. Blueprint and data mapping define the target journey. Roles, source records, approvals and exceptions are validated before qualified adoption. The goal is one BusinessOS, not another network of separate applications.
